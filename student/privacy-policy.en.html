Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026 | Developer: Xi Haifeng | 中文

This application "FlashMemo" (the "App") is developed by Xi Haifeng ("we" or "us"). We take personal information seriously. The App is designed around the principle of "your data stays on your device." Please read this policy carefully before use.

1. What Information We Collect

We do not collect, upload, or sell your personal information. The App contains no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking components. It does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across apps.

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

  • Local storage: Flashcards, notes, review history, and other content you create are stored entirely on your device. We have no access to them.
  • iCloud sync: If you enable iCloud sync, your data is synchronized between your own devices via Apple's iCloud. Encryption in transit and at rest is handled by Apple; we cannot read your iCloud data.

3. Subscriptions and Payments

Subscriptions to the App are processed through Apple's in-app purchase system. Payment information is handled by Apple; we never receive your name, payment account, or card details. To manage or cancel your subscription, go to iOS "Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions".

4. AI Features

AI features require you to configure your own API key from a third-party AI provider. When you use these features, content you actively submit (such as text, question images, or document pages) is sent directly from your device to the provider you selected, in order to generate the results you request (such as solution steps, flashcard drafts, or document summaries). This traffic does not pass through our servers. Please do not include sensitive personal information in what you submit to AI services, and review the privacy terms of your chosen provider.

Costs and usage: AI features use your own API key. Any fees are settled directly between you and your provider. We do not participate in billing, do not collect payments on anyone's behalf, and do not track or record your AI usage. Pricing and billing are governed by your provider's published terms.

Generated content: AI-generated results are shown to you for confirmation first and are only saved to your device after you confirm. You may freely edit or discard them before saving. Generated content is for study reference only — please verify it before use.

5. Diagnostic Data

  • Crash reports: When installed via TestFlight or the App Store, crash reports are collected by Apple and shared with the developer for troubleshooting (manage this under device "Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics").
  • In-app diagnostic feedback: The App offers a "send diagnostics" feature. Diagnostic information (device model, system version, app version, logs) stays on your device locally and only leaves the device when you actively choose to send it (e.g., via email).

6. Minors

The App is designed for education. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe related data has been mishandled, please contact us using the details below.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, we will announce it in the App and update the effective date.
